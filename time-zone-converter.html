About the Time Zone Converter

Plan a call or a flight across countries. Pick a date, a time and a city, and see the same moment in every major city, including daylight saving changes.

How to use it

  1. Pick the date and the time.
  2. Choose the city you are converting from and the city you want.
  3. Read the converted time, the time difference and the list of other cities.

The formula

Time there = time here + (UTC offset there − UTC offset here)

Offsets change with daylight saving time, so the date you pick matters.

Worked examples

When it is 9:00 in Riyadh on 12 October 2026, the time in India is Mon 12 Oct, 11:30. The difference is +2.5 hours.

9:00 in New York on 12 October 2026 is Mon 12 Oct, 16:00 in Riyadh, a difference of +7 hours.

Tips

  • Always pick the actual date. The gap between the Gulf and Europe or the US changes when clocks change.
  • Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and India do not use daylight saving time.
  • For meetings, pick a time that is during working hours for everyone in the list.
  • Check the weekday shown. A late evening time can be the next day in another city.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between Saudi Arabia and India?

India is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of Saudi Arabia all year round, because neither uses daylight saving time.

Why does the difference with London or New York change?

The UK and the US move their clocks in spring and autumn, so the gap changes, while the Gulf countries stay the same.

Does it handle daylight saving automatically?

Yes. Your browser holds the time zone rules, so the result reflects the date you choose.

Which cities are included?

The Gulf countries, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the UK, Europe, Turkey, Egypt, the US, Canada, Australia, Singapore and Japan.

Daylight saving can change the offset

The time difference between two locations is not always fixed throughout the year. Some regions observe daylight saving time, while others do not. A conversion should use the date of the event, not only the usual time difference between cities.

Using the Time Zone Converter wisely

For meetings, flights and online events, confirm the local date as well as the clock time. If a location changes its time-zone rules, device and operating-system databases may update at different times. Include the time zone abbreviation or city when sharing an appointment.
