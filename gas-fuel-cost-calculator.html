Estimate the gas cost of a trip.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Estimate what a trip will cost in fuel. Enter the distance in miles, your vehicle fuel economy in miles per gallon (MPG) and the current price of gas per gallon. Actual cost depends on traffic, speed, load and weather, so keep a small buffer for long drives.

The formula

Gallons needed = miles ÷ MPG. Trip cost = gallons × price per gallon.

Example

A 300-mile trip in a car that gets 28 MPG, with gas at $3.50 a gallon, uses about 10.7 gallons and costs about $37.50.
