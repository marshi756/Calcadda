Discount & Tax Calculator

Final price after a discount and sales tax.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Enter the original price, the discount percentage and your local sales tax rate. The calculator applies the discount first and then adds tax to the reduced price, which is how most stores work. Sales tax rates differ by state and city, so use the rate shown on your receipt for the most accurate result.

The formula

Sale price = price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). Final price = sale price × (1 + tax ÷ 100).

Example

A $200 item at 20% off with 8% sales tax: the sale price is $160, the tax is $12.80 and you pay $172.80.
