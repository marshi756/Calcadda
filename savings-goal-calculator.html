Savings Goal Calculator

How long it takes to reach a savings target.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Set a savings target, enter how much you have saved so far, what you add each month and the yearly return you expect. The calculator shows how long it will take to reach your goal. If the numbers can never reach the target, it will tell you. Raising your monthly deposit usually shortens the time more than hoping for a higher return.

The formula

Each month: balance = balance × (1 + annual rate ÷ 12) + monthly deposit, repeated until the balance reaches the goal.

Example

To reach $20,000 starting from $2,000 with $400 a month at 4% a year takes about 3 years and 6 months.
