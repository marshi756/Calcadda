About the Savings Goal Calculator

Saving for a home, a car or an emergency fund? Find out how long it will take, or how much to set aside each month to finish on time.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether to find the time needed or the monthly amount.
  2. Enter your goal, what you have saved so far and the expected yearly return.
  3. Enter the monthly deposit, or the number of years you have.

The formula

Monthly saving needed = (Goal − Savings × (1 + i)^n) × i ÷ ((1 + i)^n − 1)

i is the monthly return and n is the number of months.

Worked examples

To reach 20,000 starting from 2,000 and saving 400 a month at 4%, you need 3 yr 6 mo.

To build ₹10 lakh in 5 years from ₹1 lakh at 7%, you need to save ₹11,987.75 every month.

Common mistakes to avoid

People often set a goal with no date and then save irregularly. Another mistake is assuming a high return on a short goal, when savings accounts pay little. If the goal is close, use a low or zero return. Also remember to raise your monthly saving as your income grows.

Tips

  • Write the goal down with a date. Goals with deadlines get funded.
  • Keep an emergency fund separate from other goals.
  • Use a conservative return. A savings account is safer to plan with than the stock market.
  • Raise your monthly amount whenever your income rises.

Frequently asked questions

What return should I enter?

Use what your account really pays. For a bank savings account that may be 3 to 7% depending on the country. Enter 0 to ignore interest.

Can I save more than the calculator says?

Yes. Anything extra gets you there sooner. Try a higher deposit and watch the time drop.

What if the goal says it is not reachable?

The monthly deposit is too small or zero. Increase it or lengthen the time.

Is interest taxed?

Often yes, depending on where you live. This calculator ignores tax, so your real result may be a little lower.

Make the savings target practical

A target becomes easier to manage when it is split into a monthly amount and a deadline. The estimate depends on your starting balance, regular contributions and any assumed interest. If contributions are made at the beginning rather than the end of each period, the outcome may differ.

Using the Savings Goal Calculator wisely

Leave room for irregular expenses and keep emergency money accessible. If the required contribution is too high, test a later target date, a lower goal or a realistic return assumption. Interest is not guaranteed unless the account terms say so.
