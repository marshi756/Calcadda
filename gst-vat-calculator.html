About the GST and VAT Calculator

Add tax to a price or work backwards from a price that already includes tax. Pick a quick rate for your country or type your own.

Tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive prices

Adding tax to a pre-tax price is different from extracting tax from a tax-inclusive total. If the rate is 10%, adding tax to 100 gives 110; extracting the tax from 110 gives 10. The correct method depends on whether the entered amount already includes tax.

Using the GST and VAT Calculator wisely

The rate depends on the jurisdiction, product or service, exemptions and date of supply. Preset rates are shortcuts for common scenarios, not a determination that the transaction qualifies for that rate. Verify the official rate and invoice treatment before filing or issuing a tax invoice.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Choose Add tax or Remove tax.
  2. Enter the amount and pick a quick rate, or type a custom rate.
  3. Read the tax amount, the price before tax and the price with tax. For India you can also see the CGST and SGST split.

The formula

Price with tax = price before tax × (1 + rate ÷ 100)

Price before tax = price with tax ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)

Worked examples

Adding 18% GST to ₹1,000 gives ₹1,180.00. The tax is ₹180.00, split as ₹90.00 + ₹90.00 for a sale within one state.

If ₹1,180 already includes 18% GST, the price before tax is ₹1,000.00 and the tax is ₹180.00.

Tips

  • Never subtract the percentage from a tax-inclusive price. Divide by (1 + rate) instead.
  • For sales within one Indian state, tax splits equally into CGST and SGST. Across states it is IGST.
  • The VAT rate in your country may differ for food, books or medicine. Check the rate for your item.
  • Keep tax invoices. Businesses can often claim input tax credit.

Frequently asked questions

What are the GST rates in India?

Since 22 September 2025 the main rates are 5% and 18%, with 40% for certain luxury and sin goods, plus 0% for essentials and special rates such as 3% for gold. The old 12% and 28% slabs were removed.

What is the VAT rate in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Saudi Arabia charges 15% VAT and the UAE charges 5% VAT.

How do I take GST out of a price?

Divide the final price by 1 plus the rate. For 18%, divide by 1.18. Use the Remove tax option and the calculator does it for you.

Are the quick rates always current?

Tax rates change. They were last checked in October 2026. Always confirm the rate on the official tax authority website before filing or invoicing.
