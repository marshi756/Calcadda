Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcPointer ("we", "our") handles information when you visit our website.

Information you enter

The numbers and dates you type into our calculators are processed in your browser. We do not send them to our servers and we do not store them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard log data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and time of visit. This is used for security, performance and understanding how the site is used.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is usually nothing to delete, but you can contact us anytime.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
