Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcPointer ("we", "our") handles information when you visit our website.

Information you enter

The numbers, dates and text you type into our calculators are processed in your browser. We do not send them to our servers and we do not store them. The food calorie counter keeps your log only in your own browser storage on your device.

Information stored in your browser

To remember your choices, the site saves small items in your browser: your selected currency or country, your light or dark mode setting, your food log and recently loaded exchange rates. These stay on your device. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard log data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and time of visit. This is used for security, performance and understanding how the site is used.

Third-party services

To work, pages may load resources from other providers. The currency converter requests exchange rates from a public exchange rate service (open.er-api.com), and pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalisation. The technologies used and the choices available can depend on your region and the consent settings shown to you.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. Where required, advertising and consent choices are handled through the consent controls presented on the site. You can change browser cookie settings and review Google’s advertising controls; blocking cookies may affect some features.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Some information may be processed by hosting, analytics, advertising or external service providers as described above. To ask a privacy question or exercise a right that applies to you, contact us and identify the request without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
