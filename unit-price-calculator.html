About the Unit Price Calculator

Compare two products fairly. Enter each price and quantity, and see which one costs less per gram, litre or piece.

What this calculator estimates

Shelf prices are often hard to compare because packs come in different sizes. This tool divides each price by its quantity to get a price per unit. It then shows which product is cheaper per unit and how much you save by choosing it.

Formula

Price per unit = price ÷ quantity. Saving (%) = (higher unit price − lower unit price) ÷ higher unit price × 100. Both products must use the same unit, such as grams or millilitres.

Worked example

Product A costs SAR 12 for 500 g, so it is SAR 0.024 per gram. Product B costs SAR 15 for 750 g, so it is SAR 0.020 per gram. Product B is cheaper per gram. The saving is (0.024 − 0.020) ÷ 0.024 × 100, which is about 16.7%.

Step-by-step instructions

1. Enter the price of product A. 2. Enter its quantity in one unit, such as grams. 3. Enter the price and quantity of product B in the same unit. 4. Read which product is cheaper per unit, and the percentage you save.

Tips for a realistic estimate

Check the quantity on the label, because some packs show a drained weight. Remember that a bigger pack is not always cheaper per unit. Compare products of the same type and quality. Look for multi-buy offers, which change the final price per unit.

Why unit prices matter

Packaging is designed to make prices hard to compare. A large pack can look cheaper while costing more per unit, and some small packs are priced per unit at a premium. Checking the unit price helps you spend less on items you buy often, such as rice, oil, detergent or drinks, where a small saving adds up over a year.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compare litres and grams? Only compare products in the same unit. Convert first, for example 1 litre = 1000 ml. Does the calculator include offers? No, it uses the prices you enter. Is the saving always worth it? Only if you will use the whole pack before it expires, and if the quality suits your needs.

Limits of this estimate

The tool compares price per unit only. It does not measure quality, taste, or shelf life. Prices change, so check the shelf label before you buy.
