Compound Interest Calculator

How this calculator works

Compound interest means you earn returns on your earlier returns as well as on your own deposits. Enter a starting amount, a monthly deposit, an expected yearly return and the number of years to see the final balance. Time matters most: the longer the money stays invested, the faster it grows. Real returns change from year to year, so treat the result as an estimate.

The formula

Each month: balance = balance × (1 + annual rate ÷ 12) + monthly deposit, repeated for every month of the period.

Example

Starting with $5,000 and adding $200 a month at 7% a year for 20 years gives about $124,000. Of that, only $53,000 is money you put in, and the rest is growth.
