About CalcNest

CalcNest is a free website offering simple, reliable online calculators for everyday questions, including loan payments, savings growth, tips, percentages, BMI, dates and more. Our goal is to give you a clear answer in as few steps as possible, with no sign-up required.

All calculations run directly in your browser, and we do not store the numbers you enter. The results are estimates provided for general information only and are not financial, tax, health or legal advice. Please consult a qualified professional before making important decisions.

If you spot an error or would like us to add a new calculator, please get in touch.
