About CalcAdda

CalcAdda is a free website offering simple, reliable online calculators for everyday money questions, including loan EMIs, SIP returns and percentages. Our goal is to give you a clear answer in as few steps as possible, with no sign-up required.

All calculations run directly in your browser, and we do not store the numbers you enter. The results are estimates provided for general information only and are not financial, tax or legal advice. Please consult a qualified professional before making important financial decisions.

If you spot an error or would like us to add a new calculator, please get in touch.
