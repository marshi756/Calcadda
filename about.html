About CalcPointer

CalcPointer is a free website of simple, reliable online calculators for everyday money, work, health and study questions, with no sign-up required.

What you will find here

More than thirty calculators, including loans and mortgages, savings, SIP and FD, GST and VAT, a live currency converter, end-of-service benefits for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait, gratuity in India, zakat, a Hijri date converter, BMI, calories, percentages, unit conversion and student tools. Many are also available in Arabic and Hindi.

How the calculators work

All calculations run directly in your browser. We do not store the numbers you enter. Calculator pages include explanations and examples where relevant, so you can understand the assumptions behind a result. We continue to improve these guides and welcome specific reports when something is unclear or incorrect.

Accuracy

We review calculator logic and explanatory content as the site evolves. Rules such as tax rates and labour laws can change, and users should verify time-sensitive figures against the relevant official source before relying on them. Results are estimates for general information only and are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice. Please confirm important figures with an official source or a qualified professional.
