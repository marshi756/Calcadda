About the Hijri Gregorian Date Converter

Convert between the Gregorian calendar and the Hijri calendar used in Saudi Arabia (Umm al-Qura). Enter either date and see the other, with the day of the week.

Why Hijri dates may vary by location

Hijri calendars may be based on astronomical calculation or local moon-sighting announcements. As a result, a calculated date can differ by one day from the calendar officially followed in a particular country or community.

Using the Hijri Gregorian Date Converter wisely

For travel, contracts, religious observances and government deadlines, confirm the date with the relevant local authority. Use this converter as a planning aid and check whether the calendar setting matches the system or country you need.

The formula

The Hijri year has 12 lunar months of 29 or 30 days, so it is about 354 days long, roughly 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year.

This converter uses the Umm al-Qura calendar, the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia, which follows fixed astronomical calculations.

Worked examples

Tips

  • Use the Hijri date for Saudi government paperwork, contracts and many official documents that show both dates.
  • Religious dates such as the start of Ramadan or Eid depend on moon sighting and can differ by a day from the calculated calendar.
  • A Hijri month has 29 or 30 days. If a date is rejected, check the day number.
  • Hijri dates move earlier by about 11 days each Gregorian year, so Ramadan comes earlier every year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Umm al-Qura calendar?

It is the Hijri calendar system used officially in Saudi Arabia for civil purposes. It is based on astronomical calculations of the new moon rather than local sightings.

Will this match the Ramadan or Eid date announced in my country?

Not always. Announcements depend on moon sighting and local decisions, so the real start can differ by a day from the calculated date.

Why does the Hijri year have fewer days?

It has 12 lunar months, about 354 days, while the Gregorian year follows the sun and has about 365 days.

Which years does it support?

Hijri years 1300 to 1600, which covers about 1882 to 2174 in the Gregorian calendar. Gregorian dates between 1900 and 2100 are accepted.
