About the Paint Calculator

Work out how much paint a room needs. Enter the room size, subtract doors and windows, choose the number of coats, and get the litres to buy.

What this calculator estimates

This tool estimates how much paint you need for the walls of a room. It uses the room length, width and wall height to find the total wall area. It then subtracts doors and windows, multiplies by the number of coats, and divides by the coverage of your paint. The coverage on most paint tins is around 10 to 12 square metres per litre, but always check the number printed on your own tin.

Formula

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height, minus the area of doors and windows. Paint needed in litres = wall area × number of coats ÷ coverage per litre. The "round up to buy" value is the paint needed rounded up to a whole litre.

Worked example

Take a room that is 4 m long, 3 m wide and 2.7 m high, with no doors or windows to subtract, and two coats of paint at 10 m² per litre. The wall area is 2 × (4 + 3) × 2.7 = 37.8 m². Two coats need 37.8 × 2 = 75.6 m² of coverage. Divide by 10 to get 7.56 litres. Round up, and buy 8 litres.

Step-by-step instructions

1. Choose metres or feet. 2. Enter the room length, width and wall height. 3. Enter the total area of doors and windows, if you want them removed. 4. Enter the number of coats. 5. Enter the coverage per litre from your tin. 6. Read the litres needed and the rounded-up amount to buy.

Tips for a realistic estimate

Primer is a separate coat and is not included unless you count it as one of the coats. Textured walls, dark colours and old porous plaster can need more paint than the smooth-wall coverage. Add a small extra margin for mistakes and touch-ups, and keep a spare tin of the same batch.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include the ceiling? No, the calculator covers walls only. Add the ceiling area separately if you paint it. Why does the coverage matter? Coverage differs by paint type and surface, so the number on your tin gives the most accurate result. Can I use feet? Yes, choose feet and enter the sizes in feet, and the calculator converts them.

Limits of this estimate

Results are estimates for general planning. Paint coverage depends on the product, surface and application method. Check the manufacturer data sheet before buying.
