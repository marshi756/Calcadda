How this calculator works

Return on investment shows how much an investment gained or lost. Enter what you put in, what it is worth now and how many years you held it. You get the total return, the profit or loss and the annualized return, which lets you compare investments held for different lengths of time. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

The formula

Total return = (current value − amount invested) ÷ amount invested × 100. Annualized return = (current ÷ invested)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

Example

Investing $10,000 that grows to $15,000 over 4 years is a 50% total return, or about 10.7% per year.
