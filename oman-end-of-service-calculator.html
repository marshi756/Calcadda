About the Oman End of Service Calculator

Estimate the end-of-service gratuity in Oman from your last basic wage and your service dates. The calculator splits your service into the part before and the part after the new Labour Law of 2023.

Confirm which employment rules apply

End-of-service calculations can depend on the date employment began, the date it ends, the wage definition and any transitional rules that apply to the worker or contract. A general estimate may not reflect every exception or the latest administrative interpretation.

Using the Oman End of Service Calculator wisely

Keep a copy of your contract, payslips and service dates. Verify the relevant rule with Oman’s official labour resources or your employer’s HR team before using the estimate to agree to a settlement.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last basic monthly wage.
  2. Enter your start date and your last working day, and any unpaid leave days.
  3. Read the gratuity, split into service before and after the new Labour Law.

The formula

Before the new law: 15 days of basic wage for each of the first 3 years, then one month for each later year

After the new law (Royal Decree 53/2023): at least one full month of basic wage for each year of service, with part years in proportion

The last basic wage is the base for the calculation.

Worked examples

A basic wage of 400 OMR from 1 January 2020 to 1 January 2026. The part before the new law is 3.58 years, which gives OMR 832.877. The part after it is 2.42 years, which gives OMR 967.763. Total: OMR 1,800.639.

A worker who started in 2024 falls fully under the new law. 500 OMR for 2 years is OMR 1,000.000.

Common mistakes to avoid

The common mistake is applying one formula to all years. Service before the new Labour Law and service after it follow different rules, so each part is calculated separately. Another is using total salary instead of the last basic wage.

Tips

  • This calculator is for workers who are not covered by the Social Protection Law, mainly expatriate workers. Omani nationals follow the social protection system.
  • The last basic wage is used, not your total salary.
  • Service that began before the new law keeps the older formula for that earlier period only.
  • A savings scheme for end of service benefits is planned under the Social Protection Law, so check the Ministry of Labour for the current start date.

Frequently asked questions

How is end of service calculated in Oman?

For service before the new Labour Law (Royal Decree 53/2023), it is 15 days of basic wage for each of the first three years and one month for each later year. For service after it, it is at least one basic wage for every completed year.

Which date separates the old and new rules?

This calculator uses 31 July 2023, when the new law took effect. Confirm the exact date with the Ministry of Labour for your case.

Which wage is used?

The Ministry of Labour has confirmed that the last basic wage is used as the base.

Is this the official figure?

No. It is an estimate based on public Ministry guidance, last checked in October 2026. For disputes, contact the Ministry of Labour.
