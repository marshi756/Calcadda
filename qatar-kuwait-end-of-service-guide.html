Qatar and Kuwait End of Service Guide

Two Gulf systems side by side: Qatar pays a minimum gratuity, and Kuwait pays an indemnity with a cap and resignation tiers.

Qatar: the minimum gratuity

Under Article 54 of Labour Law No. 14 of 2004, a worker who completes at least one year of service is entitled to a minimum end-of-service gratuity. It is at least three weeks (21 days) of basic wage for each year of service, and part years are paid in proportion. Your contract can promise more, but not less.

Rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Qatar example

Your last basic wage is 8,000 QAR and you worked 5 years. A day is 8,000 ÷ 30 and the minimum gratuity is that day amount × 21 × 5, which is QAR 28,000.00.

Only the basic wage counts, not allowances. If your employer pays a larger gratuity under your contract or company policy, you receive the larger amount. Use our Qatar End of Service Calculator to check the legal minimum.

Kuwait: the indemnity

For monthly-paid private sector workers, Kuwait Labour Law No. 6 of 2010 (Article 51) sets the indemnity as follows.

Years of serviceIndemnity for each year
First 5 years15 days of pay (half a month)
Years after the first 5One month of pay

The total is capped at one and a half years of pay. Your last monthly remuneration is the base. Daily and hourly workers use different rates, so this guide does not cover them.

Kuwait example: contract ended by the employer

Your last monthly remuneration is 600 KWD and you worked 8 years. The first 5 years at half a month give 1,500 KWD and the next 3 years at a full month give 1,800 KWD, so the indemnity is KWD 3,300.000.

If you resign in Kuwait (Article 53)

Years of service when you resignShare of the indemnity
Less than 3 yearsNothing
3 years up to 5 yearsOne half
5 years up to 10 yearsTwo thirds
10 years or moreThe full indemnity

Kuwait example: resigning after 4 years

With 600 KWD and 4 years of service, the full indemnity would be KWD 1,200.000. Because you resigned between 3 and 5 years you receive half, which is KWD 600.000.

Common mistakes

  • In Qatar, using total salary instead of basic wage.
  • In Kuwait, applying the half-month rate to every year, when it changes to a full month after 5 years.
  • In Kuwait, forgetting that resignation under 3 years gives nothing.
  • Forgetting that a contract may promise more than the law.

Frequently asked questions

Does Qatar pay more than 21 days per year?

The law sets 21 days of basic wage per year as the minimum. Many contracts offer more.

Is there a cap in Qatar?

The minimum rule has no cap in this guide. Check your contract for any limit.

What if I resign in Kuwait after 2 years?

You receive nothing under the resignation tiers, because the first tier starts at 3 years.

Which pay counts in Kuwait?

Your last monthly remuneration. Check your contract to see which parts of pay are included.

Are these official calculations?

No. They are explanations. For a dispute, contact the labour authority in your country.
