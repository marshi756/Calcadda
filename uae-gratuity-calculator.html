About the UAE Gratuity Calculator

Estimate your end-of-service gratuity in the UAE from your last basic salary and your service dates, using the 21 and 30 day rule and the two-year cap.

Verify the employment arrangement

UAE end-of-service benefits can depend on the applicable labour framework, contract type, eligible basic wage, service duration and reason employment ends. Different employment arrangements or special regimes may have different rules, so a single formula will not cover every case.

Using the UAE Gratuity Calculator wisely

Check your contract and current official UAE government or labour-authority guidance. Ask HR for a written breakdown that states the wage basis, service period and any deductions. Use this estimate to check arithmetic, not as a final legal determination.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last basic monthly salary, without allowances.
  2. Enter your start date and last working day, and any unpaid leave days.
  3. Read the gratuity, the days of pay counted and whether the cap applied.

The formula

First 5 years: 21 days of basic pay for each year. After 5 years: 30 days for each year.

Daily wage = basic ÷ 30. The total cannot be more than two years of wages.

Worked examples

A basic salary of 5,000 for 3 years: 5,000 ÷ 30 × 21 × 3 = AED 10,500.00.

A basic salary of 10,000 for 6 years: five years at 21 days plus one year at 30 days gives AED 45,000.00, which is 135 days.

Tips

  • Only the last basic salary counts. Housing, transport and other allowances are not included.
  • At least one year of continuous service is needed.
  • Unpaid leave is not counted in your service period, so enter those days.
  • Under a termination for serious misconduct, gratuity may be lost, so check the rules for your case.

Frequently asked questions

How is gratuity calculated in the UAE?

21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years and 30 days for each year after that, based on the last basic salary and capped at two years of wages.

Does resignation reduce the gratuity?

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 most sources state that resignation no longer reduces it. Some older unlimited contracts were treated differently, so check your contract and the MOHRE guidance.

When must the employer pay it?

Within 14 days of the end of the contract.

Does this include DIFC or free zone schemes?

No. Some free zones such as DIFC have their own savings schemes. This calculator follows the standard mainland rule, last checked in October 2026.
