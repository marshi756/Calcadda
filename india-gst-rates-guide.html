India GST Rates Guide: 5%, 18% and 40% Explained

The current GST slabs in India, what changed, and how to add or remove GST from a price without mistakes.

What changed in 2025

From 22 September 2025, India simplified its GST structure. The main rates are now 5% and 18%, with 40% for certain luxury and sin goods, and 0% for essentials. A special rate of 3% applies to gold. The older 12% and 28% slabs were removed.

Rates in this guide were last checked in October 2026. GST rates can change, so confirm the rate for your item on the official GST portal or with your accountant.

RateWhere it is used
0%Essentials that carry no GST
3%Gold
5%The lower standard slab
18%The main standard slab
40%Certain luxury and sin goods

Which rate applies to a product depends on its classification. This guide does not list products, because the lists change. Look up your item in the official rate schedule.

How to add GST to a price

Price with GST = price before tax × (1 + rate ÷ 100)

You sell an item for ₹1,000 and the rate is 18%. The tax is ₹180.00 and the total with GST is ₹1,180.00.

A ₹5,000 item at 5% GST gives a total of ₹5,250.00.

A ₹10,000 item at 40% GST gives a total of ₹14,000.00.

How to remove GST from a price

If the price already includes GST, do not subtract the percentage. Divide instead.

Price before tax = price with GST ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)

A bill shows ₹1,180 with 18% GST included. The price before tax is 1,180 ÷ 1.18, which is ₹1,000.00, and the GST is ₹180.00.

Taking 18% off ₹1,180 gives the wrong answer, because 18% of ₹1,180 is more than the real tax. Always divide.

CGST, SGST and IGST

When the buyer and seller are in the same state, GST is split equally into CGST (central) and SGST (state). When they are in different states, the whole amount is charged as IGST. For the ₹1,000 example above, a same-state sale shows ₹90 CGST and ₹90 SGST, while an inter-state sale shows ₹180 IGST. The total is the same either way.

Common mistakes

  • Subtracting the percentage instead of dividing when the price already includes GST.
  • Using the old 12% or 28% rates, which were removed.
  • Applying one rate to a bill that has items under different rates.
  • Forgetting that gold has its own 3% rate.

Frequently asked questions

What are the GST rates in India now?

The main rates are 5% and 18%, with 40% for certain luxury and sin goods, 0% for essentials and 3% for gold. The old 12% and 28% slabs were removed in September 2025.

How do I calculate GST on a price?

Multiply the price before tax by the rate and add it. For 18%, multiply by 1.18.

How do I remove GST from a bill?

Divide the bill by 1 plus the rate. For 18%, divide by 1.18.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST each take half of the GST on a sale within a state. IGST takes the whole GST on a sale between states.

Is this list always up to date?

Rates can change. Confirm on the official GST portal before filing or invoicing.
