Frequently asked questions

Are these formulas the same in India, the USA and Canada?

Almost always yes. The mathematics is the same everywhere. What differs is the vocabulary, such as trapezium in India and the UK versus trapezoid in the US, and the order in which topics are taught.

What is BODMAS and PEMDAS?

They are two names for the same order of operations: brackets or parentheses first, then orders or exponents, then division and multiplication, then addition and subtraction.

Can I print this sheet?

Yes. Use the Print button and choose Save as PDF on your phone or computer.

Which units should I use?

Use the units your exam asks for. Most of the world uses metric units, while the US also uses feet, inches and pounds. The unit converter can help.

How to use this formula sheet

Identify the quantity you need, write down the known values with their units, and select the formula that relates them. Substitute values only after converting them to compatible units. Keep intermediate steps so you can spot an arithmetic or unit error.

Using the Math Formula Sheet wisely

A formula may require conditions that are easy to overlook—for example, a geometry formula may assume a regular shape, and a statistics formula may assume a particular data definition. Use the sheet as a study aid and follow the notation and methods required by your course or examination board.

Formulas are standard school mathematics. Always follow your own syllabus and teacher for the version your exam expects.
