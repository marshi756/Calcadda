UAE Gratuity Guide: How End of Service Is Calculated

How gratuity works for employees in the UAE, in plain language, with examples that use the same formula as our calculator.

What gratuity means in the UAE

Gratuity is the end-of-service payment an employer owes after an employee has completed at least one year of continuous service. It is separate from your last salary and your unused leave. This guide covers employment on the UAE mainland. Free zones such as DIFC and ADGM can have their own schemes, so check yours if you work there.

Rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

The formula

Gratuity is based on your last basic salary. Allowances such as housing and transport are not included. A day of pay is your basic salary divided by 30.

Years of serviceGratuity for each year
First 5 years21 days of basic salary
Years after the first 530 days of basic salary

The total cannot be more than two years of your wages. Unpaid leave days are taken out of your service period, and you need at least one year of continuous service to qualify.

Example 1: 3 years of service

Your basic salary is 5,000 AED and you worked 3 years. A day is 5,000 ÷ 30. Gratuity is that day amount × 21 × 3, which is AED 10,500.00.

Example 2: 7 years of service

Your basic salary is 6,000 AED and you worked 7 years. The first 5 years give 6,000 ÷ 30 × 21 × 5, which is 21,000 AED. The next 2 years give 6,000 ÷ 30 × 30 × 2, which is 12,000 AED. The total gratuity is AED 33,000.00.

What counts as salary

Only the basic salary counts. If your payslip shows a total of 10,000 AED made of 6,000 basic plus 4,000 in allowances, the calculation uses 6,000. This is the single most common reason for a gap between what employees expect and what they receive.

The cap

Gratuity is limited to two years of wages. For most people this only matters after many years of service. The calculator shows when the cap was applied.

Payment time and records

The law requires the employer to settle your dues, including gratuity, within 14 days of the end of the contract. Keep your contract, payslips, bank transfers and the final settlement letter in case you need to check the figures later.

Resigning or being terminated

Older rules reduced the gratuity for an employee who resigned in the early years. Sources describe the current position differently, so our calculator shows the full formula without a resignation reduction. If you are resigning, confirm your exact entitlement with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) or a labour lawyer before you rely on the figure.

Common mistakes

  • Using total salary instead of basic salary.
  • Forgetting to remove unpaid leave days from the service period.
  • Counting 30 days for every year, when the first 5 years use 21 days.
  • Assuming the cap never applies after very long service.

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible for gratuity in the UAE?

Employees who complete at least one year of continuous service with the same employer on the mainland.

Which salary is used?

The last basic salary. Allowances are not included.

How many days per year do I get?

21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years and 30 days for each year after that.

Is there a maximum?

Yes. The total gratuity cannot be more than two years of wages.

Does this apply in free zones?

Not always. Free zones can have their own rules, so check with your free zone authority.
