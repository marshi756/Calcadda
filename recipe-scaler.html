About the Recipe Scaler

Cook for more or fewer people. Enter the original servings, the servings you want, and your ingredient amounts, and get the new quantities.

What this calculator estimates

This tool scales each ingredient of a recipe in the same proportion as the number of servings. If a recipe makes 4 servings and you want 6, each ingredient is multiplied by 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5.

Formula

Scale factor = servings you want ÷ original servings. New ingredient amount = original amount × scale factor.

Worked example

A recipe for 4 people uses 200 g of rice and 100 ml of oil. You want to cook for 6 people. The scale factor is 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5. The new amounts are 200 × 1.5 = 300 g of rice and 100 × 1.5 = 150 ml of oil.

Step-by-step instructions

1. Enter the number of servings the original recipe makes. 2. Enter the number of servings you want. 3. Enter each ingredient amount for the original recipe. 4. Read the new amounts. You can leave the third ingredient empty if the recipe has only two.

Tips for a realistic estimate

Not every ingredient scales in a straight line. Spices, salt and leaveners are often increased more slowly, so taste as you go. Cooking time and pan size can also change, especially for baked goods, so check doneness rather than relying only on the original time.

Scaling for groups and leftovers

Scaling is useful for big gatherings, for halving a family recipe, or for planning meals for a week. When you scale up a lot, consider cooking in batches, because very large pots and pans heat unevenly. When you scale down, check whether the recipe still makes sense with very small amounts, especially for eggs or spices.

Keeping track of quantities

Write the new amounts next to the original ones before you start cooking. This avoids mistakes when you measure, and it helps you check that every ingredient has been scaled in the same way. If you cook the same recipe often, save both versions so you can reuse them.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for baking? It works for the quantities, but baking needs care with pan size and time. Can I scale down? Yes. Enter a smaller number of servings and the amounts will fall. Does it convert units? No, enter the amounts in the same unit you use in the recipe.

Limits of this estimate

The calculator is a simple proportion. It does not change cooking time, temperature or pan size. Use your judgement and taste as you cook.
