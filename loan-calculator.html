Loan Payment Calculator

Monthly payment for a mortgage, car or personal loan.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Enter the amount you borrow, the yearly interest rate and the loan term in years. The calculator splits the loan into equal monthly payments, so each payment covers the interest for that month plus part of the balance. It suits fixed-rate mortgages, car loans and personal loans. Property tax, insurance and lender fees are not included, so your real monthly cost may be higher.

The formula

Monthly payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of months.

Example

A $300,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years costs about $1,896 per month. Over the full term you would repay roughly $683,000, which means about $383,000 of that is interest.
