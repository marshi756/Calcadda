About the Saudi End of Service Calculator

Estimate your end-of-service award in Saudi Arabia from your last wage and your service dates, for termination, expiry of contract or resignation.

Worked example: 7 years of service

Take an employee with a last wage of SAR 8,000 (basic plus regular allowances) who has worked 7 years.

  1. First 5 years: half a month a year, so 0.5 × 8,000 × 5 = SAR 20,000.
  2. Remaining 2 years: a full month a year, so 8,000 × 2 = SAR 16,000.
  3. Total award if the employer ends the contract: SAR 36,000.
  4. If the employee resigns after 7 years, the award is reduced to two thirds: 36,000 × 2/3 = SAR 24,000.

The calculator shows these steps for you, so you can check the result against your own contract and final payslip.

How resignation changes the award

Under Article 85 of the Saudi Labour Law, the award is reduced when the employee resigns. The reduction depends on how long the employee worked:

  • Less than 2 years: no end of service award.
  • 2 years to less than 5 years: one third of the award.
  • 5 years to less than 10 years: two thirds of the award.
  • 10 years or more: full award.

Example: an employee who resigns after 4 years on SAR 8,000 gets one third of the award for 4 years, which is about SAR 5,333. The same employee would get the full SAR 16,000 if the employer ended the contract.

Which wage is used

The award is calculated on the last actual wage. This normally means the basic salary plus regular fixed allowances such as housing and transport. Overtime, one-off bonuses and irregular payments are usually not included. If your allowances are unclear, check your employment contract and the last payslip.

When there is no award

If an employee is dismissed under Article 80 for serious misconduct, the end of service award is not payable. Choose the Article 80 option in the calculator to see this result. Other cases, such as contracts ending because of force majeure, may follow different rules. Confirm these with HRSD or a qualified employment lawyer.

What this calculator does not include

This tool estimates only the end of service award. It does not include unused annual leave payout, salary owed for the last month, notice pay, or deductions. A complete final settlement may include more than the award, so add those amounts separately when you check what you are owed.

Verify with official sources

Labour rules can change. Before you rely on a figure for a real decision, check the Saudi Labour Law and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) website. For a dispute or a complex case, speak to HRSD or a qualified lawyer.

More questions about the Saudi end of service award

Is end of service paid to Saudi and non-Saudi workers?

The Saudi Labour Law applies to private sector employees in Saudi Arabia, and the award rules apply to eligible workers whatever their nationality. Check your own contract and the current HRSD guidance for your situation.

Do I get the award after 6 months?

The award is not paid for less than 2 years of service when you resign. If the employer ends the contract, the award is calculated pro rata for the part years, so the result depends on the length of service and the reason for ending.

What if my contract was renewed several times?

Service is usually counted from the start date on the first contract, unless the rules or your records say otherwise. Keep your contracts and check the start date carefully.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last monthly wage, which means basic salary plus regular allowances.
  2. Enter your start date and your last working day.
  3. Choose how employment ended and read the award.

The formula

First 5 years: half a month's wage for each year

After 5 years: one full month's wage for each year

Resignation: nothing under 2 years, one third from 2 to 5 years, two thirds from 5 to 10 years, and the full award at 10 years or more

Worked examples

Four years at 8,000 SAR with the contract ended by the employer: 0.5 × 8,000 × 4 = SAR 16,000.00.

The same four years at 6,000 SAR but resigning: the full award would be SAR 12,000.00, and one third of it, SAR 4,000.00, is paid.

Twelve years at 10,000 SAR: five years at half a month plus seven years at a full month gives SAR 95,000.00.

Tips

  • The wage used is your actual last wage including regular allowances, not only the basic salary.
  • Part years count in proportion, so months beyond full years are not lost.
  • Resigning just before a threshold, such as 5 or 10 years, can reduce the award sharply. Check the dates.
  • Dismissal for the serious reasons in Article 80 can lose the award, and there is none during probation.
