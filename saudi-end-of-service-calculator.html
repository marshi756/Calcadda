About the Saudi End of Service Calculator

Estimate your end-of-service award in Saudi Arabia from your last wage and your service dates, for termination, expiry of contract or resignation.

Check the Saudi employment details

The estimated benefit can depend on the applicable Saudi labour rules, wage components, total service, the reason employment ended and any exceptions that apply to the worker. A calculator result should not be treated as a binding legal entitlement.

Using the Saudi End of Service Calculator wisely

Confirm your start date, final working date, eligible wage and reason for separation. Compare the result with current Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development guidance and your written settlement. If your case includes unpaid leave, resignation or a dispute, seek official clarification.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last monthly wage, which means basic salary plus regular allowances.
  2. Enter your start date and your last working day.
  3. Choose how employment ended and read the award.

The formula

First 5 years: half a month's wage for each year

After 5 years: one full month's wage for each year

Resignation: nothing under 2 years, one third from 2 to 5 years, two thirds from 5 to 10 years, and the full award at 10 years or more

Worked examples

Four years at 8,000 SAR with the contract ended by the employer: 0.5 × 8,000 × 4 = SAR 16,000.00.

The same four years at 6,000 SAR but resigning: the full award would be SAR 12,000.00, and one third of it, SAR 4,000.00, is paid.

Twelve years at 10,000 SAR: five years at half a month plus seven years at a full month gives SAR 95,000.00.

Tips

  • The wage used is your actual last wage including regular allowances, not only the basic salary.
  • Part years count in proportion, so months beyond full years are not lost.
  • Resigning just before a threshold, such as 5 or 10 years, can reduce the award sharply. Check the dates.
  • Dismissal for the serious reasons in Article 80 can lose the award, and there is none during probation.

Frequently asked questions

How is end of service calculated in Saudi Arabia?

Under Article 84, you get half a month's wage per year for the first five years and one month's wage per year after that, based on your last wage.

What if I resign?

Article 85 reduces the award: nothing before 2 years, one third from 2 to 5 years, two thirds from 5 to 10 years, and the full amount at 10 years or more.

Which cases give the full award even if I leave?

Certain cases, such as leaving due to force majeure or the circumstances in Article 81, can give the full award. Use the full-award option for those.

Is this an official calculation?

No. It is an estimate based on Articles 84 and 85, last checked in October 2026. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development provides an official service, and a labor lawyer can confirm your case.
