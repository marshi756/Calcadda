About the Zakat Calculator

Work out your zakat on savings, gold, silver, investments and trade goods, after deducting debts due now, and see whether you are above the nisab.

Review the assets included in your calculation

Zakat assessment can depend on asset type, ownership, valuation date, nisab threshold, lunar-year requirements and the treatment of debts or business assets. Not every possession is treated identically, and scholarly approaches can differ for particular circumstances.

Using the Zakat Calculator wisely

Use the calculator as an organising aid. Review the assumptions for cash, gold, silver, investments, trade goods and liabilities, and verify current market values. If your circumstances are complex, consult a trusted qualified scholar or relevant zakat authority; this result is not a fatwa.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter today's gold price per gram (and silver if you use the silver nisab) in your currency.
  2. Add your cash, gold and silver, investments, business stock and money owed to you.
  3. Enter the debts you must pay now, then read the zakat due.

The formula

Net wealth = cash + gold + silver + investments + trade goods + money owed to you − debts due

Nisab (gold) = 85 grams × gold price. Zakat = 2.5% of net wealth if it is at or above the nisab.

Worked examples

With gold at 300 SAR per gram, cash of 50,000 and 100 g of gold, net wealth is SAR 80,000.00. The nisab is SAR 25,500.00, so zakat due is SAR 2,000.00.

Tips

  • Zakat is due on wealth held for one full lunar year (hawl) that stays above the nisab.
  • Many scholars use the silver nisab because it is lower. Others use gold. Follow the opinion you trust.
  • Personal items such as the home you live in are not counted. Views differ on worn jewelry, so ask a scholar.
  • Pay zakat to those eligible, and consider paying before Ramadan ends if that is your habit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab?

The nisab is the minimum wealth above which zakat becomes due. It is commonly set at 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver.

What rate is zakat?

2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, which is one fortieth.

Why does the calculator need the gold price?

Because the nisab is measured in gold or silver. Enter today's local price per gram so the threshold is accurate. The price is not filled in because it changes daily.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It is a calculation aid. Opinions differ between schools on items like jewelry and shares, so please confirm with a qualified scholar.
