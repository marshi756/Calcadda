Due Date Calculator

Estimated due date from the first day of your last period.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period and the calculator estimates your due date by adding 280 days, which is 40 weeks. This is the standard method known as Naegele rule. It assumes a regular 28-day cycle, and only a small share of babies arrive exactly on the estimated date. Your doctor or midwife can confirm your date with an ultrasound.

The formula

Estimated due date = first day of last period + 280 days.

Example

If the last period began on August 1, 2026, the estimated due date is May 8, 2027.
