About the Kuwait End of Service Calculator

Estimate your end-of-service indemnity under the Kuwait private sector labor law, for monthly-paid employees, with the resignation tiers for open-ended contracts.

Employment details can change the result

Kuwait end-of-service indemnity may depend on whether the worker is paid monthly or under another wage basis, length of service, contract type and the circumstances in which employment ends. A simplified calculator cannot determine every exception.

Using the Kuwait End of Service Calculator wisely

Check your contract, start date, last working date and the wage basis used by payroll. Compare the estimate with current official labour guidance and ask the relevant authority or a qualified adviser if your situation involves resignation, dismissal, unpaid leave or a disputed wage amount.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last monthly wage.
  2. Enter your start date and last working day.
  3. Choose whether the employer ended the contract or you resigned, then read the indemnity.

The formula

First 5 years: 15 days of wage for each year. After 5 years: one month of wage for each year.

The total cannot exceed one and a half years of wages.

Worked examples

A wage of 600 for 7 years: 75 days for the first five years plus 3 months for the next two gives KWD 2,700.000.

Resigning after 4 years from an open-ended contract pays half of the full KWD 1,200.000, which is KWD 600.000.

Tips

  • The resignation tiers apply to open-ended contracts: nothing under 3 years, half from 3, two thirds from 5 and the full amount from 10 years.
  • This calculator is for monthly-paid employees. Daily, weekly or hourly workers have different rates.
  • Use your full last wage as paid by the employer.
  • Keep your contract and payslips. They decide disputes.

Frequently asked questions

How is the Kuwait indemnity calculated?

Under Article 51, monthly-paid employees get 15 days of wage for each of the first five years and one month for each year after, capped at one and a half years of wages.

What happens if I resign?

Under Article 53, an employee on an open-ended contract gets nothing under three years, half from three to five years, two thirds from five to ten years and the full indemnity after ten years.

Is the cap always applied?

Yes. The total indemnity cannot exceed one and a half years of wages. The result tells you if the cap was reached.

Is this exact?

It is an estimate based on Law No. 6 of 2010, last checked in October 2026. Confirm with your employer or the Public Authority for Manpower.
