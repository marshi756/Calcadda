About the Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days, the total days you have lived, and how long until your next birthday.

How to use it

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Leave the second date as today, or pick another date to find your age on that day.
  3. Read your age, total days and the countdown to your next birthday.

The formula

Age = the difference between the two dates in years, months and days

Days to next birthday = the next occurrence of your birthday − today

Worked examples

Born on 15 June 1995, on 11 October 2026 you are 31 years, 3 months, 26 days. That is 11,441 days, and your next birthday is in 247 days (Tuesday).

Common mistakes to avoid

People often count age by subtracting birth year from the current year. That is wrong until the birthday has passed. Another common slip is using the wrong date format, such as month and day swapped, so always check the date shown back to you. For legal or school cut-offs, use the exact cut-off date as the second date.

Tips

  • Use another date to check eligibility, such as age on the first day of a school year or exam.
  • Government forms often need age on a cut-off date. Set the second date to that day.
  • People born on 29 February celebrate on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years.
  • Total days is handy for milestones, such as 10,000 days.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated exactly?

From your birth date to the chosen date, counting full years first, then full months, then the remaining days.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the second date to any date after your birth date.

Why does the day count differ between sites?

Some sites count the end date and some do not, and months have different lengths. This calculator uses calendar months.

Which weekday was I born on?

It is shown at the bottom of the results.

Worked example

Suppose your date of birth is 15 March 1990 and you check your age on 7 November 2026. From 15 March 1990 to 15 March 2026 is 36 years. From 15 March to 15 October 2026 is 7 months. From 15 October to 7 November is 23 days. So your exact age is 36 years, 7 months and 23 days, which is 13,386 days in total.

Why the total days can be useful

The total number of days is useful for loan eligibility, school or job age cut-offs, pension planning, and for fun milestones like a 10,000-day birthday. The calculator counts calendar days, so a leap day is included in the total.

Check the date carefully

Official age rules often depend on the date of birth recorded on a passport, national ID or school certificate. If your record uses a different calendar, such as the Hijri calendar in Saudi Arabia, convert the date first with the Hijri converter, then confirm the official age rule with the relevant authority.

Choose dates carefully

The result depends on the calendar dates you enter. If the end date is earlier than the birth date, the dates are reversed or invalid for an age calculation. For official forms, use the date of birth shown on your identity document and follow the form’s rules for completed years.

Using the Age Calculator wisely

Example: someone born on 15 March 2000 is 26 years old on 15 March 2026. A day before that birthday, the completed age is still 25. The extra days and months shown by this tool are calendar-based, not a substitute for an official age determination.

How the age calculator works

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it with today’s date and shows your exact age in years, months and days. It accounts for month lengths and leap years.

Common uses

  • Forms, exams and applications that ask for exact age
  • Checking age for benefits, licences or eligibility rules
  • Finding the precise gap between two dates when combined with a date-difference tool
