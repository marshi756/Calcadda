How this calculator works

Choose a date of birth and the calculator shows your exact age in years, months and days, plus the total number of days you have been alive. It uses today as the end date, so the result changes each day. It is useful for forms, school or sports age limits and milestone birthdays.

The formula

Age = today minus date of birth, counted in whole years first, then the remaining months and days.

Example

Someone born on June 15, 1995 is, on June 15, 2026, exactly 31 years, 0 months and 0 days old.
