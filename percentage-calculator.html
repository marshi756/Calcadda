How this calculator works

This tool answers the most common percentage questions in one place: what is a percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, and what a number becomes after adding or subtracting a percent. It is handy for discounts, tips, grades, taxes and test scores.

The formula

A% of B = A ÷ 100 × B. A is what percent of B = A ÷ B × 100. B plus A% = B × (1 + A ÷ 100).

Example

15% of 2,000 is 300. Adding 15% to 2,000 gives 2,300, and subtracting 15% gives 1,700.
