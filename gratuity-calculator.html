About the Gratuity Calculator India

Estimate the gratuity you can receive when you leave a job in India, using your last drawn basic salary plus dearness allowance and your years of service.

Check eligibility before relying on the estimate

Indian gratuity calculations commonly depend on eligible service, last drawn eligible wages and the statutory formula, with exceptions and special rules in some circumstances. Whether a person qualifies can depend on employment type, service history and applicable law.

Using the Gratuity Calculator India wisely

Use the estimate to prepare questions for payroll, not as a final settlement statement. Verify the wage components included, completed service period, any applicable ceiling and the rules currently in force with an official source or qualified professional.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your last drawn monthly Basic plus DA.
  2. Enter your completed years of service and any extra months.
  3. Choose whether your employer is covered by the gratuity law, then read the gratuity and the tax-free portion.

The formula

Covered by the law: Gratuity = last drawn salary × 15 ÷ 26 × years of service

Not covered: Gratuity = last drawn salary × 15 ÷ 30 × completed years

A period of more than six months is counted as a full year.

Worked examples

₹40,000 Basic plus DA for 3 years: 40,000 × 15 ÷ 26 × 3 = ₹69,230.77.

₹60,000 for 13 years gives ₹4,50,000.00.

₹50,000 for 10 years and 8 months counts as 11 years and gives ₹3,17,307.69.

Tips

  • Only Basic plus dearness allowance counts as wages, not your full CTC.
  • The tax-free limit is 20 lakh rupees for private employees. Amounts above it are taxable.
  • Gratuity usually needs five years of continuous service, except on death or disability.
  • Under the new labour codes in force since 21 November 2025, fixed-term employees can qualify after one year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the gratuity formula in India?

Last drawn salary × 15 ÷ 26 × completed years of service, where salary means Basic plus DA. The 26 stands for working days in a month.

How many years are needed to get gratuity?

Five years of continuous service for most employees. Fixed-term employees are eligible after one year under the new labour codes, and death or disability are exceptions.

Is gratuity taxable?

Up to the limit, which is 20 lakh rupees, it is exempt for private sector employees. The excess is added to your income.

Is this exact for my company?

It is an estimate. Your employer's policy, whether the law applies and how wages are defined can change the amount. Confirm with HR.
