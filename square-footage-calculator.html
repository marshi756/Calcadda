Square Footage Calculator

Area of a room or rectangle in feet, inches, yards, meters, centimeters or kilometers.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Choose a unit, then enter the length and width of a room, lot, wall or any rectangle. The calculator converts everything to meters internally and shows the area in square feet, square inches, square yards, square meters, square centimeters, square kilometers and acres, along with the perimeter. If you also enter a height, it shows the volume in cubic feet, cubic meters and liters. Use the same unit for all three measurements. For irregular rooms, split the space into rectangles and add the results.

The formula

Area = length × width. Volume = length × width × height. Conversions: 1 foot = 0.3048 meters, 1 square foot = 0.0929 square meters, 1 acre = 4,046.86 square meters.

Example

A room that is 12 feet by 10 feet has an area of 120 square feet, which is about 13.3 square yards or 11.15 square meters. The same room entered as 365.76 cm by 304.8 cm gives the same area. With a ceiling height of 8 feet, its volume is 960 cubic feet.
