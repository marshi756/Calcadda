About the Square Footage and Area Calculator

Measure a room, a plot or a shape. Get the area in square feet, square meters, square yards (gaj), acres and hectares, plus volume if you add a depth.

How to use it

  1. Pick a shape and the unit you measured in.
  2. Enter the length and width, the base and height, or the radius.
  3. Optionally add a depth or height to get the volume.

The formula

Rectangle area = length × width

Triangle area = ½ × base × height. Circle area = π × radius².

Worked examples

A room 12 ft by 10 ft is 120 sq ft, which is 11.15 sq m or 13.33 gaj.

A circular area with a 3 m radius is 28.27 sq m.

Common mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is mixing units, for example a length in feet and a width in inches. Pick one unit for both. Another is forgetting waste: flooring and paint need extra beyond the measured area. For irregular rooms, split the shape into rectangles and triangles, calculate each and add them together instead of guessing.

Tips

  • Measure each wall and floor carefully. For L-shaped rooms, split the room into rectangles and add the areas.
  • Remember that carpet and tiles need extra for waste, often 5 to 10%.
  • Property listings in India use sq ft, gaj or bigha, and the meaning of bigha varies by state.
  • Use the volume option for concrete, soil or water tank estimates.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square footage?

Multiply the length in feet by the width in feet. A 12 ft by 10 ft room is 120 square feet.

How many square feet in a gaj?

One gaj (square yard) is 9 square feet.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply by 0.0929. The calculator shows both.

Can I measure in inches or centimeters?

Yes. Pick the unit you measured in and the results still appear in all common area units.

Measure the shape before converting units

For a rectangle, area is length multiplied by width. Measure both dimensions in the same unit first; if a room is irregular, divide it into simpler shapes, calculate each area and add them. Subtract large openings only if the purpose of the measurement calls for it.

Using the Square Footage and Area Calculator wisely

Flooring and paint purchases may require extra material for cuts, waste or pattern matching. Land units and local terms such as gaj can vary by context, so verify the conversion standard used in a property document or local transaction.
