About the Tip and Bill Split Calculator

Work out the tip, add it to the bill and split it fairly. Round each share up if you want a clean number.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill amount and the tip percentage.
  2. Enter how many people are sharing.
  3. Tick round up if you want whole-number shares, then read each person's amount.

The formula

Tip = bill × tip % ÷ 100

Each person pays = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people

Worked examples

An 85 bill with an 18% tip split two ways: the tip is $15.30, the total is $100.30, and each person pays $50.15.

A £60 bill with a 10% tip split four ways is £16.50 each.

Common mistakes to avoid

A frequent mistake is tipping on a bill that already has a service charge, which means paying twice. Another is splitting the total evenly when people ordered very different amounts. For uneven orders, calculate shared items here and add each person's own items on top.

Tips

  • Tipping customs differ. In the US 15 to 20% is common, while many countries expect 5 to 10% or a service charge already included.
  • Check the bill first. Some restaurants add a service charge automatically.
  • Use round up when paying in cash to avoid change.
  • Calculate the tip on the bill before tax if you prefer.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

It depends on the country and the service. 15 to 20% is common in the US, 10% is common in the UK and Europe, and in the Gulf a service charge is often added already.

Should I tip on the tax too?

Either is acceptable. Many people tip on the pre-tax amount.

How do I split unevenly?

Split the shared items with this calculator, and add each person's own items separately.

Does it work in any currency?

Yes. Pick your currency at the top of the page and the amounts are formatted for it.

Split the bill transparently

A tip percentage is commonly calculated from the bill amount before or after tax depending on local custom and personal preference. Decide which base you want to use before calculating. When splitting a bill, the total is divided by the number of people after the tip is added.

Using the Tip and Bill Split Calculator wisely

Example: a 10% tip on a 50 bill is 5, making 55 before any other charges. Check whether a service charge has already been added so you do not unintentionally tip twice. Rounding each person’s share can create a small difference from the exact total.
