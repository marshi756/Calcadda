Tip & Split Calculator

Tip and per-person share of a bill.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Type the bill, choose a tip percentage and enter how many people are sharing. You get the tip amount, the total and the share for each person. In the United States 15% to 20% is a common tip at sit-down restaurants, but customs vary by place and service. Tip is calculated on the bill before you add it, so check whether tax is included.

The formula

Tip = bill × tip % ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Each person pays = total ÷ number of people.

Example

On an $85 bill with an 18% tip split between 2 people, the tip is $15.30, the total is $100.30 and each person pays $50.15.
