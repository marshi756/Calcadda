Days between two dates.

How this calculator works

Pick two dates and see how many days, weeks and approximate months are between them. This helps with deadlines, trip planning, project schedules and countdowns. By default the end date is not counted. Tick the box if you want both the first and the last day included, which adds one day.

The formula

Days = absolute difference between the two dates in days (add 1 if both days are counted). Weeks = days ÷ 7.

Example

From January 1 to December 25 in 2026 is 358 days, or 359 days when both the start and end day are counted.
