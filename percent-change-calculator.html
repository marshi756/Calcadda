Percent Change Calculator

Percentage increase or decrease between two numbers.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Percent change tells you how much a value went up or down compared with where it started. Enter the old value and the new value. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease. It is useful for prices, salaries, website traffic, savings and test scores.

The formula

Percent change = (new value − old value) ÷ |old value| × 100.

Example

A price that rises from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase. Going back from 100 to 80 is a 20% decrease, because the starting point is different.
