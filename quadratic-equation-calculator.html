About the Quadratic Equation Calculator

Enter a, b and c to solve a quadratic equation. You get the roots, the discriminant, the vertex and whether the parabola opens up or down.

How to use it

  1. Write your equation as ax² + bx + c = 0.
  2. Enter a, b and c. Use negative numbers where needed.
  3. Read the roots, the discriminant and the vertex.

The formula

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a

Discriminant D = b² − 4ac. If D > 0 there are two real roots, if D = 0 one repeated root, and if D < 0 two complex roots.

Worked examples

x² − 3x − 10 = 0 has D = 49, so the roots are x₁ = 5, x₂ = -2.

x² + 2x + 5 = 0 has D = −16, so the roots are complex: x = -1 ± 2i.

Tips

  • Always move every term to one side so the right side is zero.
  • Check your answer by putting a root back into the equation.
  • The sum of the roots is −b/a and the product is c/a, a useful way to verify.
  • If a = 0 the equation is not quadratic. It becomes a simple linear equation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quadratic formula?

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. It gives the solutions of ax² + bx + c = 0 for any a not equal to zero.

What does the discriminant tell me?

Its sign tells you how many real roots exist: positive means two, zero means one, negative means none (two complex roots).

What is the vertex of a parabola?

It is the turning point, at x = −b ÷ 2a. It is the minimum if a is positive and the maximum if a is negative.

Can I solve by factoring instead?

Yes, when the roots are simple numbers. The formula always works and is useful when factoring is hard.

Read the discriminant and roots

For an equation ax² + bx + c = 0, the discriminant is b² − 4ac. A positive discriminant gives two distinct real roots, zero gives one repeated real root, and a negative discriminant gives a pair of complex roots. The coefficient a must not be zero for the equation to be quadratic.

Using the Quadratic Equation Calculator wisely

Check the result by substituting each root into the original equation. Small decimal differences can occur because displayed values are rounded. When completing homework or an exam, show the formula and steps required by your teacher rather than submitting only the numerical answer.
