About the Hourly to Salary Calculator

Compare a job paid by the hour with a salaried offer. Convert in either direction and see the monthly, weekly and daily pay as well.

How to use it

  1. Choose hourly to salary or yearly salary to hourly.
  2. Enter the rate or the salary.
  3. Set your hours per week and the number of paid weeks per year.

The formula

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year

Hourly rate = yearly salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks)

Worked examples

At 25 an hour for 40 hours, 52 weeks, you earn $52,000.00 a year, which is $4,333.33 a month.

A salary of 60,000 for a 40 hour week is $28.85 an hour.

Common mistakes to avoid

Many people multiply the hourly rate by 40 hours and 52 weeks without checking unpaid leave, which overstates pay. Others compare a gross hourly job with a net salary offer. Compare gross with gross, and remember that salaried roles may include benefits, paid holidays or bonuses that an hourly job does not.

Tips

  • If you get unpaid time off, reduce the paid weeks to see a realistic yearly figure.
  • Remember to compare gross with gross. Taxes differ by country.
  • Count overtime separately. It is often paid at a higher rate.
  • A salary job with long hours can work out to a lower hourly rate than it first looks.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are in a work year?

A 40 hour week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours. Fewer paid weeks or hours lower the figure.

Is the result before or after tax?

Before tax. It is the gross amount from the rate and hours you enter.

How do I include paid holidays?

Paid holidays are included when you count 52 paid weeks. Unpaid holidays should be taken out.

Does it work for part-time jobs?

Yes. Enter your real weekly hours, such as 20, and the weeks you work.

Salary conversions require a schedule assumption

Annualising an hourly wage assumes a consistent number of paid hours and working weeks. Paid leave, unpaid breaks, overtime, bonuses and seasonal hours can change actual earnings. A salary-to-hourly conversion is therefore a comparison tool, not a payroll statement.

Using the Hourly to Salary Calculator wisely

For a fair job comparison, calculate base pay separately from benefits, pension contributions, overtime eligibility and paid leave. Confirm whether the quoted salary is gross or take-home pay and whether the working week in the calculator matches the employment contract.
