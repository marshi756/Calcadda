How this calculator works

Turn an hourly wage into weekly, monthly and yearly pay, or the other way around when planning a budget. Enter your hourly rate, the hours you work each week and the weeks you are paid each year. The result is pay before taxes and deductions, so your take-home pay will be lower.

The formula

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year. Monthly pay = yearly pay ÷ 12.

Example

$25 an hour for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks is $52,000 a year, or about $4,333 a month before tax.
