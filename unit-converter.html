Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, volume, area, temperature, speed and time between metric and US units.

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this converter works

Pick the unit you are starting from, type a value and the converter instantly shows the same amount in every other unit of that kind. It covers length (millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards, miles), weight (milligrams, grams, kilograms, pounds, ounces, stone, tons), volume (milliliters, liters, gallons, quarts, pints, cups and more), area, temperature, speed and time. Because the units are grouped, you can use it for height, width, thickness and distance too.

The formula

Converted value = value × (factor of the starting unit ÷ factor of the target unit), where every factor is expressed in a base unit such as meters, kilograms or liters. Temperature uses its own formulas: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and K = °C + 273.15.

Example

1 kilogram is about 2.2046 pounds and 35.274 ounces. 100 centimeters is 1 meter, 39.37 inches and 3.2808 feet. 98.6 °F is 37 °C and 310.15 K.
