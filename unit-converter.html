The formula

Value in new unit = value × (factor of old unit ÷ factor of new unit)

Temperature uses its own formulas, for example °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32.

Worked examples

170 Centimeters (cm) = 5.577428 Feet (ft), which is also 5 ft 6.93 in.

10 Tola (11.66 g) = 116.638 Grams (g). Gold in India and the Gulf is often sold by the tola.

37 Celsius (°C) = 98.6 Fahrenheit (°F), normal body temperature.

Common mistakes to avoid

Mixing US and UK gallons is the most expensive error, because they differ by about 20%. Another is using the wrong ton: the metric ton, the US short ton and the UK long ton are all different. For temperature, remember that Celsius and Fahrenheit are not simple multiples, so use the converter instead of a rule of thumb.

Tips

  • For height, convert centimeters to feet and inches with the extra row shown.
  • 1 US gallon is 3.785 litres but 1 UK gallon is 4.546 litres. Choose the right one.
  • Square yard is called gaj in India. 1 gaj is 9 square feet.
  • Double check whether a source means US or UK units.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert cm to feet and inches?

Divide centimeters by 2.54 to get inches, then divide by 12 for feet. The calculator shows the feet and inches for you.

What is a tola?

A traditional unit of weight used in South Asia and the Gulf, mainly for gold. One tola is about 11.66 grams.

What is the difference between MPG (US) and MPG (UK)?

The UK gallon is larger, so a UK MPG figure is about 20% higher for the same car.

Are the conversions exact?

They use standard definitions and are accurate to several decimal places.

Keep units consistent

A unit conversion changes the way a quantity is expressed; it does not change the underlying quantity. For example, one metre equals 100 centimetres, and one kilogram is approximately 2.20462 pounds. Temperature conversions use offsets as well as scaling, unlike simple length conversions.

Using the Unit Converter wisely

Check the unit labels before copying a result into a form, recipe, drawing or technical calculation. Some customary units have regional variants—for example, US and UK gallons are different. Use the specific standard required by the task.
