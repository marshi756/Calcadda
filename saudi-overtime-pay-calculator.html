About the Saudi Overtime Pay Calculator

Work out what your overtime hours are worth in Saudi Arabia. The law pays each overtime hour at your normal hourly wage plus 50% of your basic hourly wage.

Overtime eligibility is separate from arithmetic

An overtime estimate depends on recorded hours, the wage components used, the applicable premium and whether the hours qualify under current rules. Employee category, rest days, public holidays and contract details may affect treatment.

Using the Saudi Overtime Pay Calculator wisely

Keep time records and payslips, and compare the calculation with current Saudi labour guidance or your employer’s HR policy. This tool performs arithmetic on the inputs you provide; it does not determine legal eligibility or replace an official payroll review.

Rates and rules in this guide were last checked in October 2026.

How to use it

  1. Enter your basic monthly wage and any fixed allowances.
  2. Enter your normal working hours per day (the default is 8) and the overtime hours worked.
  3. Read the overtime rate per hour and the total overtime pay.

The formula

Hourly wage = (basic + allowances) ÷ 30 ÷ daily hours

Overtime rate = hourly wage + 50% × (basic ÷ 30 ÷ daily hours)

Worked examples

On a basic wage of 6,000 and allowances of 2,000, the normal hourly wage is SAR 33.33 and the overtime rate is SAR 45.83. Ten overtime hours are worth SAR 458.33.

With basic 2,400 and allowances 1,800, the basic hourly wage is SAR 10.00 and the overtime hour is paid at SAR 22.50.

Tips

  • The 50% premium is worked out on your basic wage, not on the whole salary, so allowances raise the hourly wage but not the premium.
  • Keep a record of the days and hours you work extra, with start and end times.
  • Hours worked on a rest day or an official holiday are treated as overtime under the same rule.
  • Normal daily hours can be shorter, for example during Ramadan for Muslim employees, so enter the hours that apply to you.

Frequently asked questions

What does Article 107 of the Saudi Labor Law say?

The employer must pay an additional wage for overtime hours equal to the hourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage.

How is the hourly wage worked out?

Divide the monthly wage by 30 days, then by the normal working hours in a day, which is usually 8.

Does overtime use basic salary only?

The premium uses the basic wage. Some employers use basic salary for the whole calculation, so check your contract and company policy.

Is this an official calculation?

No. It is an estimate based on Article 107, last checked in October 2026. For disputes, contact the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
