About the Gold Rate Calculator

Find the value of your gold and the price per gram, per tola and per ounce for every karat, in your own currency. This calculator does not fetch a live gold price. Enter the current 24K price per gram yourself for an accurate value.

How to use it

  1. Pick your currency, then enter the weight of your gold and its unit: grams, tola, troy ounce or kilograms.
  2. Choose the purity: 24K, 22K, 21K or 18K. Add making charges if you are pricing jewellery.
  3. Read the value of your gold and the price per gram for each karat. If you know today's local 24K price, type it in the last box and the calculator uses that price.

The formula

Price per gram of 24K gold = international price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1035, converted to your currency

Price of another karat = 24K price × karat ÷ 24

Value = weight in grams × price of that karat × (1 + making charges ÷ 100)

Worked examples

Suppose 24K gold is 400 SAR per gram. Then 21K is SAR 350.00 per gram, and 10 grams of 21K gold is worth SAR 3,500.00.

Suppose 24K gold is ₹12,000 per gram (an illustrative price). One tola of 22K jewellery with 10% making charges comes to ₹1,41,132.03, and it contains 10.69 g of pure gold.

Common mistakes to avoid

The usual mistake is comparing a jeweller's price for 22K jewellery with the price of 24K bullion. They are different things. Another mistake is forgetting making charges and taxes, which can add a noticeable amount to jewellery. For investment, compare the price of the pure gold content, not the price of the ornament.

Tips

  • The price shown is an international market price. Jewellers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and India add their own margin, so shop prices are usually higher.
  • In India, retail gold also carries 3% GST and import costs, so local rates are higher than a converted international price.
  • Making charges and stone weight change the final price of jewellery. Ask for the gold weight and the making charge separately.
  • If you plan to calculate zakat on gold, use the same karat and weight in the zakat calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 21K gold?

The number shows how many parts out of 24 are pure gold. 24K is almost pure (99.9%), 22K is 91.6% gold, 21K is 87.5% and 18K is 75%. Lower karats are harder and are mixed with other metals.

How much is a tola of gold?

One tola is about 11.66 grams. It is a traditional unit still used for gold in India, Pakistan and the Gulf.

Why is my jeweller's price different from this calculator?

This calculator uses the international market price converted to your currency. Jewellers add their buying and selling margin, making charges and local taxes or duties.

Do I need to enter the gold price myself?

Yes. This calculator does not load a live gold price. Type today's 24K price per gram from your local dealer or market source, and the calculator works out the value for every karat and unit.

Understand purity and market-rate differences

Gold value depends on weight, purity, reference price and currency conversion. Jewellery prices can also include making charges, retailer margins and taxes. A calculated metal value is not the same as a shop’s selling price or a buyback offer.

Using the Gold Rate Calculator wisely

Confirm the rate timestamp, purity stamp and unit (gram or tola) with the seller before buying or selling. Local prices can differ by shop and market conditions, and currency rates may be approximate when a live rate service cannot be reached.

How the gold rate calculator works

Enter the weight, purity (karat) and currency. You enter the 24K price per gram, and the calculator scales it to each karat. You can override the price with your local jeweller’s rate.

Karat and purity

  • 24K — pure gold (about 99.9%)
  • 22K — common for jewellery in India and the Gulf (about 91.7%)
  • 21K and 18K — lower purity, often used in some regions and designs
