BMI Calculator

Body mass index from weight and height (US units).

Estimates only, not professional advice.

How this calculator works

Body mass index compares your weight with your height. Enter your weight in pounds and your height in inches. In general, a BMI under 18.5 is labeled underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 healthy, 25 to 29.9 overweight and 30 or more obese. BMI is only a screening number. It does not measure body fat or muscle, so talk to a doctor for personal health advice.

The formula

BMI = 703 × weight in pounds ÷ (height in inches)².

Example

A person who weighs 160 lb and is 67 inches tall has a BMI of about 25.1.
