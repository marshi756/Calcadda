Contact Us

Have a question, found a mistake, or want a new calculator added? We would love to hear from you.

Email: arshim370@gmail.com

We read every message and usually reply within a few days. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information by email.

What to include in a report

Please include the page URL, a short description of what you expected, the units and example inputs, and the browser/device type if the issue is visual. Never include account passwords, identity numbers or private financial documents.

Reporting an error

If a result looks wrong, tell us the calculator name, the numbers you entered and the result you expected. That helps us fix it quickly.

What to include in your message

If you found a mistake, please include the page address, the input you used, the result you got, and the result you expected. Screenshots are helpful. If you want a new calculator, tell us the country, the rule or formula, and an example. Clear messages get faster and more useful replies.

What we can and cannot help with

We can explain how a calculator works, fix errors in our tools, and consider new calculators. We cannot give personal tax, legal, medical or investment advice, and we cannot check your individual employment contract, loan or bank statement. For those, please speak to a qualified professional or the official authority.

Privacy and sensitive information

Please do not send passwords, bank account numbers, national ID numbers, passport details, or medical records by email. Our calculators run in your browser, so you do not need to send us your numbers to get a result.
