Free EMI, SIP and Percentage Calculators

Enter your numbers and get instant results. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; every calculation runs in your browser.

EMI Calculator

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay each month to repay a loan. Each instalment covers part of the principal and part of the interest. A lower interest rate or a shorter tenure reduces the total interest you pay, so it is worth comparing several tenures before you borrow.

SIP Calculator

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) invests a fixed amount at regular intervals, and returns compound over time. The result shown is an estimate based on a constant rate of return. Actual returns vary with the market and are not guaranteed.

Percentage Calculator

Use this calculator to work out discounts, taxes, tips or commissions. Enter the percentage and the amount to see the percentage value, the total after adding it, and the total after subtracting it.
