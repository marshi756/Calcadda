About CalcPointer

CalcPointer is a free collection of online calculators for everyday money, work, health and study questions. Each tool runs in your browser, needs no account and explains the formula behind the result.

The site is designed for people in India and the Gulf as well as the UK, Europe, North America and Australia. That is why you will find the end-of-service benefits calculators for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait, GST and VAT, gratuity in India, zakat, SIP and FD tools and a Hijri date converter beside the classic loan and percentage tools, with metric and imperial units and many currencies.

Frequently asked questions

Is CalcPointer free?

Yes. Every calculator is free to use, with no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising.

Is my data safe?

Your numbers are processed in your browser. Calculators do not send what you type to a server, and the food log stays on your device.

Do the calculators work outside the US?

Yes. Pick your country at the top of the page and the currency, units and the most useful tools adapt. There are tools for India and the Gulf, and the site is also available in Arabic and Hindi.

Are the exchange rates live?

The converter attempts to retrieve rates from an external provider. If the provider is unavailable, approximate fallback rates may be used and clearly identified in the result.

Are the results exact?

They are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Tax rates, labor laws and other rules change, so confirm important figures with an official source or a qualified professional.
