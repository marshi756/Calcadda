About the CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Convert CGPA to percentage or back. Pick the rule your board or university uses, because the formula is not the same everywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose CGPA to percentage or percentage to CGPA.
  2. Choose the conversion rule that your board or university uses.
  3. Enter the value and read the result and the rule used.

The formula

CBSE: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

General: CGPA × 10. VTU: (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. Mumbai University: 7.1 × CGPA + 12.

Worked examples

A CGPA of 8.4 under the CBSE rule is 79.8%.

The same CGPA under the VTU rule is 76.5%.

A percentage of 75 under the CBSE rule is a CGPA of 7.89.

Tips

  • Always follow the rule printed by your own board or university. Applications for jobs and admissions often ask for the official conversion.
  • Many Indian universities simply multiply CGPA by 10, but several use their own formula.
  • Keep your marksheet. Some institutions give an official equivalence certificate.
  • This tool is for a 10 point scale. A 4 point GPA works differently.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage for CBSE?

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.

Why are there different formulas?

Boards and universities set their own rules, so the same CGPA can map to a different percentage depending on where you studied.

Can I convert percentage back to CGPA?

Yes. Choose Percentage to CGPA and the same rule is applied in reverse.

Is the converted figure official?

No. It is an estimate based on the published rule. For official purposes ask your institution for a certificate.

Confirm your institution’s conversion rule

There is no single universal formula that converts every CGPA scale to a percentage. Some institutions multiply by a fixed factor, while others publish their own conversion table or use a different maximum grade point. Use the method required by your school, university or application form.

Using the CGPA to Percentage Calculator wisely

Example: on a 10-point scale, a simple ×9.5 method turns 8.0 CGPA into 76%, but this is valid only where that rule is officially accepted. Keep the institution’s conversion policy with your records when submitting marks for admissions or employment.
